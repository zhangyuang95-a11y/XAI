01 / WAREHOUSE

Warehouse · 仓库

Deliver parcels together. Start by asking about the first potential collision decision. Later sidebar checks cover detours, collisions and the first charging decision.

WASD / arrow keys to move; Space to wait. 配送包裹，先学习询问 AI 的避碰决定；后续提示显示在右侧。

02 / PONG

Pong · 合作接球

Catch balls with your teammate. Team balls need you both. Prompts offer explanations of the selected team-ball catch.

You are blue; AI is orange. A / D to move; Space to wait. 你是蓝色球拍，AI 是橙色球拍。

03 / KITCHEN

Kitchen · 厨房

Prepare dishes together. Every five steps, choose whether to see an explanation of the next AI decision.

WASD to move; E to interact; Space to wait. 移动、备料并与 AI 交接，每五步可选择是否查看解释。

The demo starts at Task 2, turn 0. The guide appears at the first relevant decision; in Warehouse this may be the opening position. 体验从 Task 2 第 0 步开始，在第一个相关决策节点引导提问；仓库可能在开局就触发。
