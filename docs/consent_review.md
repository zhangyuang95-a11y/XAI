# Participant information and consent — review copy

The researcher confirmed on 22 September 2026 that IRB-2025-996 applies to this experiment. This static review copy does not enroll participants; recruitment remains subject to the configured study entry.

**Project Title:** Understanding Human Communication and Collaboration with AI Systems

**NTU-IRB Ref No.:** IRB-2025-996

You are invited to participate in a research study on **how people communicate and collaborate when solving tasks with an AI teammate**. This study is conducted by **Dr. Yewen Pu** at Nanyang Technological University, together with research team members Yuang Zhang and Hanxuan Ju.

This study will take approximately **10 minutes** of your time per session, including setup, a tutorial or demonstration, three collaborative task rounds, and a short survey. You will use a **web interface** and will be randomly assigned to **one of three games: Warehouse, Cooperative Pong, or Cooperative Kitchen**. You will complete Task 1, Task 2, and Task 3 within your assigned game; you will not be asked to play all three games. Depending on your assigned condition, you may be able to ask questions and receive explanations during **Task 2 only**.

During the collaborative game tasks, we will record your actions, scores, interaction timing, and, where applicable, your questions and the AI teammate's explanations. We will also collect your survey responses. **No audio or video recording is required.** Questions submitted through the explanation feature may be processed by **DeepSeek through its API**. Please do not include personal information in your questions.

Your participation in this study is completely voluntary, and you have the right to withdraw from the study at any time without any penalty. You may skip any survey questions you do not wish to answer. If you do not wish to continue, you may close your browser. If you joined through Prolific, please also return your submission on Prolific if you withdraw.

Your participation in this research will be kept confidential. Research data will be stored in **coded (pseudonymous) form**: each session will be identified by a unique, system-generated study-session code rather than your name or other directly identifying details. Your Prolific ID will be held separately for compensation and duplicate-participation checks. It will not be included in publications, shared datasets, or analysis files released outside the research team. Results will be analysed in aggregate and may be disseminated through academic publications and conference presentations. This study will contribute to understanding how people communicate and collaborate effectively with AI systems.

Session data, including action logs, questions and explanations, and survey responses, will be stored in a **PostgreSQL database managed by Neon in Singapore**. The storage and AI-processing arrangements may involve transferring your study data outside Singapore or your country of residence. If you use the explanation feature, your questions, recent questions and answers, and relevant gameplay information will be sent to the AI service provider to generate explanations. The application does not add your study-session code or authentication token to these requests. However, any identifying information you include in your questions will also be transmitted. Please do not include your name, contact details, or other identifying information in your questions.

You will receive a **fixed payment of £3.00** for completing this study, according to the platform's payment rules. Payment does not depend on your game score, and there is no performance-based bonus in this pilot. You may skip any survey question you do not wish to answer. There are **no foreseeable risks** to individuals participating in this study beyond those encountered in ordinary online activities.

If you have questions about this project, please contact Hanxuan Ju or Yuang Zhang via the research team at [JUHA0001@e.ntu.edu.sg](mailto:JUHA0001@e.ntu.edu.sg), or **Dr. Yewen Pu** at [yewen.pu@ntu.edu.sg](mailto:yewen.pu@ntu.edu.sg).
